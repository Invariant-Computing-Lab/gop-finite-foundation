# Universal claim and finite claims

U retains the intended theory-of-everything explanatory claim: physical processes and their embedded observers, representations, communications and actions should have a common physically constrained law architecture. A version-frozen candidate makes finite obligations inspectable; it does not prove the universal quantifier.

For a declared regime R=(boundary, preparation domain, intervention set, observer, reference units, finite time T, tolerance epsilon, resource budget B), a constructive bounded claim requires one fixed adapter and parameter estimation procedure, selected before evaluation, to produce observation laws within epsilon of the independent target for every declared intervention. No future trajectory may be encoded in the initial condition. For deterministic scored examples use maximum position discrepancy or mean squared error as explicitly registered; these are not the same as a uniform distributional guarantee.

Hierarchy: representability -> construction under budget -> identifiable estimation -> physically adequate predictions -> human/clinical relevance. None implies all the subsequent levels. The scalar learned benchmark tests only a fixed adaptation procedure for three known structures. The degree7 witness rejects exact autonomous closure under this monomial grammar; it is not a theorem that every finite-time epsilon approximation or every hidden-state extension fails.

B-ALL is a deliberately strong, exactly falsifiable bounded representability subclaim. For xdot=-x-x^7 and the highest allowed scalar monomial exponent m<=4, its derivative has degree m+6>2m. Thus the chosen finite grammar cannot close exactly. This is an openly constructed analytic witness, not a blind natural discovery. Its failure does not justify automatically expanding the grammar.

The candidate forbids negative quantum rates/probabilities, undeclared future-observation feedback, exact arbitrary high-degree autonomous closure within the fixed monomial budget, undeclared nonlocal couplings, instantaneous FIFO receipt and recovery of observationally indistinguishable latent states without added assumptions. Several are imported mathematical/causal constraints, not novel physical predictions.

No new cross-sector nomological constraint has been obtained. Shared syntax and separately supplied coefficients do not establish one physical law. The nomological task remains to derive structural constants, admissible couplings, metric/causal structure and observable restrictions without inserting each target law independently. Newtonian inverse-square embedding, Lorentz covariance, dynamical metric/Einstein limits, interacting quantum fields, full thermodynamics of apparatus and intrinsic agency are named unresolved obligations. Finite negative outcomes stay attached to this release; future work may request a versioned revision instead of silently narrowing 'everything'.
