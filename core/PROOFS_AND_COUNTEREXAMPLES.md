# Conditional mathematics and preserved failures

These are human-readable elementary derivations, not proof-assistant certificates.

1. **Cubic/quintic exact manifold.** For r=2 or4, L=x^r and f=-ax-bx^(r+1), DL f=-ra x^r-rb x^(2r). Substitution w=x^r yields the stored quadratic drift. Differentiating e=w-x^r using the *extended* x equation gives edot=-r(a+bw)e. Local uniqueness preserves e=0. On this manifold x*f=-ax²-bx^(r+2)<=0; with a>0 and even r the state is bounded and exists globally. Initial lift error is representation error, not a new physical state. When w>-a/b it contracts instantaneously; no off-manifold global stability theorem is claimed.

2. **Ito correction.** With additive sigma noise and L=x², Ito gives dw=(-2aw-2bw²+sigma²)dt+2sigma*x dW. The *same* Brownian increment drives x and w; independent lift noise would break the transformation. The exact SDE preserves w=x². Euler-Maruyama generally does not exactly preserve the manifold; measured residual vs step size is a discretization diagnostic. V=x² has generator -2ax²-2bx⁴+sigma², supplying the scalar nonexplosion bound. For a quintic noisy lift x⁴, the correction is 6sigma²*x² and transformed diffusion4sigma*x³; an extra x² coordinate would be necessary. This extension is permitted by the generic budget but NOT numerically tested here.

3. **Duffing closure and work.** qdot=v, vdot=-alpha*q-beta*q³-delta*v+u. For w=q² and r=qv: wdot=2r and rdot=v²-alpha*w-beta*w²-delta*r+q*u. All terms use the same quadratic operator. E=v²/2+alpha*q²/2+beta*q⁴/4 obeys Edot=u*v-delta*v². For positive alpha, nonnegative beta/delta and bounded u, Young/Gronwall bounds E on finite horizons, hence global continuation. Conservation is claimed only when u=delta=0. Integrating work does not close the externally powered apparatus.

4. **Finite monomial obstruction.** Let xdot=-x-x⁷ on a scalar block with no extra physical states or clocked jumps. Every auxiliary coordinate is x^m with2<=m<=4; let M be the largest exponent including the physical x (M=1 if no lift). The exact derivative of x^M is -M*x^M-M*x^(M+6). Any quadratic polynomial in the selected coordinates has degree at most2M in x. Since M+6>2M for M<=4, equality on an open interval is impossible. The target is confining gradient flow V=x²/2+x⁸/8. This contradicts B-ALL, not unrestricted quadratization, nonmonomial encodings or a finite epsilon claim. The mismatch of the fixed quintic adapter against the degree7 analytic trajectory is illustrative, not minimization over all possible F2 adapters.

5. **Known no-lift failures retained.** Archived F0 drift degree<=2 has best uniform cubic error1/4 on[-1,1]. Archived F1 degree<=3 has quintic error1/16, from Chebyshev alternating extrema. Nonlinear derived lifts were forbidden in those old statements, so their new use cannot retroactively repair F0/F1.

6. **Observation equivalence.** For linear dx=A x dt+G dW and y=H x+noise, x*=c x, H*=H/c, G*=cG and transformed initial distribution give the identical y law. With unknown scale no method identifies c. For A=-aI and H=(1,1), preparations(1,0) and(0,1) have identical entire noiseless records; this is stronger than instantaneous rank loss. Distinct decay rates may make a rank-one sensor dynamically observable and are not subject to that particular proof. Invertible calibration retains distinctions; a constant map cannot distinguish these physical states even where full sensing can. Physical interventions and lossy coarse graining are not coordinate relabelings.

7. **Composition.** Normalized causal stochastic kernels compose by iterated integration. Finite CP trace-nonincreasing instrument branches whose sum is TP compose into normalized outcome probabilities. Classical conditional registers record outcomes only. A prescribed memory/action policy and external work source are not proof of intrinsic autonomy. No human meaning or wellbeing follows from synthetic regulation.

8. **Quantum restriction.** A Hermitian-jump dissipator annihilates identity: JIJ-{J²,I}/2=0. Its autonomous reduced semigroup is unital. A lowering jump is not unital; adding it is an explicit F2 library extension, not a derivation or retroactive success of F0. Pauli/Born/GKSL structure is imported. A local TP map on one factor leaves the other marginal unchanged by partial-trace cyclicity; the Bell control checks this consequence.

9. **Bits and propagation.** For binary a,b, a+b-2ab equals XOR and lies in{0,1}. Erasing b maps8 preparations to4 outputs, whereas retaining b in a provenance register preserves8 distinctions. Sequential noncommuting gates must retain order. No heat follows without a physical reset model. Synchronous nearest-neighbour lattice maps propagate initial influence at most one edge per tick by induction; continuous diffusion tails and Lorentz covariance are different claims.
