## What this package preserves from sources

- F0-G is an explicit restricted generator/instrument candidate. F0-P is an observation-space predictor, not its identified flow.
- Fixed no-lift degree bounds admit the source's cubic/quintic counterexamples; their negative results must remain.
- Bounded quadratization/lifting is an investigated option, not a new physics discovery and not retroactive success for F0.
- Good single-step forecasting can coexist with poor long-run behavior, observer ambiguity and unphysical restrictions.
- Physics-law consistency checks do not establish derivation of the imported law or a verified universal theory.
- The conceptual architecture keeps RARI, OCIL/OCIR, AFC and SACRA distinct. Validation stays within relevant program branches.

## What is newly requested here

- A single F2 specification directly responsible for empirical forecasts, with parameter/adapter/inference/solver traceability.
- A finite finalization cycle: at most one development-only scientific repair, then a frozen specification, one final evaluation and terminal closeout.
- Explicit mathematical lifting costs, constraint-manifold and stochastic-transformation checks; separate finite claim classes.
- A version-frozen foundation with an implemented future-study intake, append-only evidence and no silent core mutation.
- Independent release completeness and claim-validity statuses.

These new rules are design requirements, not descriptions of capabilities already implemented in the old archive. The source audit's reported numerical checks are not re-performed merely by including its files here.
