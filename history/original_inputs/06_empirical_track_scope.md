# Empirical track scope: F0-P is not F0-G

The finite predictor track is explicitly an observation-record forecasting experiment. F0-P uses a complete degree-two polynomial dictionary in the current and previous calibrated, causally imputed observation records, current scheduled intervention, and a bounded public context vector. Coefficients are fitted jointly across the training worlds **within each family**, and are fixed for development selection, calibration and locked testing. Across families the dictionary rule and procedure are shared; numerical coefficients are not. The latent reference states and privileged simulator parameters do not enter the learner. Configured public physical settings are inputs equally available to every comparator.

This predictor is a finite surrogate associated with the larger research question. It is not a discretization or identified estimator of the F0-G restricted continuous generator. Polynomial continuous vector fields generally have non-polynomial finite-time flow maps, and observing only a projection can create memory and nonlinear observation effects. Thus failure or success of F0-P alone does not falsify, establish, or recover F0-G. Direct generator and quantum-channel obstructions are evaluated separately.

## Fixed information and comparison scope

All fitted comparators use the same world splits, observations, scheduled controls, public context, target scale and development selection objective. Linear ridge, bounded sparse transition regression (degree two plus individual coordinate cubes), a small tanh MLP and persistence are eligible comparators. The comparator with lowest development mean one-step capped normalized MSE is fixed separately for each family before locked data are generated. The sparse comparator is inspired by sparse equation discovery but it is not continuous-time SINDy, and its incomplete cubic library must not be called general cubic mechanism discovery. Each tuned model receives exactly three settings. Parameter counts differ and are disclosed. Search counts are equal; elapsed computation and expressiveness are not identical. No domain-specific Kalman, learned symplectic, quantum-channel identification or intervention-design baseline was fitted in this track.

The reference generators preserve observation masks, raw sensor records, calibration metadata and truth separately. This finite forecast track does not consume a separate missingness mask: forecasts concern the causally imputed records, not latent physical states or unobserved sensor measurements. All comparators share this limitation. Separate missingness-aware inference, missing-versus-zero scientific discrimination and parameter recovery are NOT_RUN here. Known invertible sensor calibrations are supplied by the experimental apparatus; applying them is not discovery of an observer-invariant representation. Rank loss, unknown gain, clipping and missingness remain unresolved in the recorded inputs.

Dense quadratic products do not implement locality, quantum positivity, conservation or an endogenous agency boundary. No observation-space interaction graph is assumed. Therefore ablations of those constraints, forced eleven-dimensionality or normative scalarization are not applicable to this predictor and must not be filled with irrelevant numerical tables. The executed ablations remove the previous observation record or public context; the constant model tests only the adequacy of an uninformative forecast.

## Units, estimands, calibration and limitations

Input and target means and standard deviations are fitted only on training data. Every loss uses the candidate-independent training-target standard deviation. Physical units are not added before nondimensionalization. Regression/network coefficient counts exclude scalers; the development ledger separately records stored numeric scalar counts and checkpoints retain all learned preprocessing statistics.

The primary independent unit is a world. Three nested stochastic realizations and all time/coordinate errors are averaged within each world. Loss is capped normalized MSE, with standardized residuals clipped at magnitude one million for numerical overflow accounting. Divergent sixteen-step trajectories are explicitly flagged, rather than interpreted as physical failure. The horizon and origins are fixed in the empirical protocol. The same actual scheduled control values are supplied throughout an open-loop sixteen-step prediction; future observations or reference states are not supplied.

The interval multiplier is the maximum standardized one-step absolute error over each calibration world's three nested seeds, time steps and coordinates. Eight calibration worlds and target coverage 80% yield conformal rank ceil(9*0.8)=8. At 90% the finite-sample rank would be nine, requiring an infinite interval with only eight calibration worlds. Intervals are coordinate boxes with half-width q times the training target standard deviation. The marginal split-conformal argument requires exchangeable independent worlds. Locked shift populations and predetermined heterogeneous sensor/mechanism strata do not automatically meet this assumption; empirical world-trajectory coverage and mean width are reported descriptively, without an unconditional coverage guarantee.

Ten primary family comparisons use paired world differences and 20,000 bootstrap replicates stratified by the predeclared locked test type. Resampling preserves the fixed stratum counts. The 99.5% percentile intervals use a Bonferroni nominal allocation across ten comparisons, but a sixteen-world bootstrap does not supply an exact finite-sample familywise guarantee. The estimand is the fixed test mixture, not all possible mechanisms or a population weighted by natural frequency. A difference of 0.01 squared training-standard-deviation units is a preregistered engineering tolerance, not a physically or clinically established effect-size threshold. Results not supporting either a margin-exceeding advantage or disadvantage are INCONCLUSIVE. A comparative rejection is about F0-P's stated adequacy criterion, not the truth of F0-G or the impossibility of a future unification theory.

Freezing and locked-data generation occur through explicit sequential phases and archived hashes. The agents share a filesystem and can see the code and split definitions. This is procedural separation, not blinded investigation, external preregistration or cryptographic sealing. Neither new parameter draws nor independent noise seeds are described as unseen-family generalization. Mechanism, observer, intervention and composition shifts are identified in reference metadata, and not all such shifts are available in every family. The deterministic programmed-memory family tests a new composition of known gates, not general computation.
